# LaunchCheck Terms of Service

_Last updated: 1 October 2026_

LaunchCheck provides automated, read-only checks of public websites.

## Permitted use

Use LaunchCheck only on public HTTP(S) pages that you are permitted to access. Do not use it to probe private networks, bypass access controls, overload third-party services, or perform unlawful activity.

## Nature of results

LaunchCheck reports automated signals and sampled checks. Results can be incomplete, stale, or affected by redirects, bot protections, hosting configuration, dynamic rendering, or other conditions.

v0.1 does not execute JavaScript, click controls, submit forms, or perform visual browser inspection. A LaunchCheck result is not a guarantee that a site is secure, accessible, legally compliant, performant, or bug-free.

## Availability

The service may change, be rate-limited, or be unavailable without notice during this early release.
