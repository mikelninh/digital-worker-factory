# Commercial Metrics

Optimise the business around value that can be measured without rewarding unsafe behavior.

## Primary

- collected revenue;
- successful bounded workflows;
- client time/cost saved against a declared baseline;
- first-pass completion / exception rate;
- human-review burden;
- customer retention after a measured pilot.

## Safety counter-metrics

A commercial metric is invalid without its counter-metric:

- speed **and** error rate;
- automation rate **and** human-boundary violations;
- workflow volume **and** failed/blocked rate;
- response time **and** privacy incidents;
- cost saved **and** service-quality complaints.

## Billing source

Capability Outcome Receipts provide a consistent machine-readable basis for successful execution counts. They are an auditable input to billing, not permission to auto-charge without the customer contract.
