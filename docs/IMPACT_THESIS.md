# Impact Thesis

Digital Worker Factory should remove repetitive operational friction while making agent authority more inspectable than ordinary ad-hoc automation.

Success means:

- people spend less time on low-value repetitive handling;
- customer response/completion quality improves or remains stable;
- exceptions reach humans reliably;
- consequential authority stays bounded;
- privacy and auditability do not worsen;
- customers can tell which work actually succeeded.

Commercial success is collected cash plus measured client value, safety gated.
