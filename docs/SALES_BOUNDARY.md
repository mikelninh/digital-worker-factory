# Sales Truth Boundary

Sales material may say:

- the runtime has explicit capability, policy, approval and audit boundaries;
- successful/blocked/failed/shadowed outcomes can be distinguished by a common receipt;
- HausPilot has deterministic/synthetic evaluation evidence;
- a customer pilot can measure one workflow before/after.

Sales material may not say:

- an agent is autonomous beyond the configured capability/approval model;
- synthetic evals prove production accuracy;
- a prepared draft equals an external action;
- blocked/failed/shadowed calls are successful completed work;
- customer data is a secondary monetisation asset.
