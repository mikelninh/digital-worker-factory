# Portfolio Role — Digital Worker Factory

Digital Worker Factory is the portfolio's near-term commercial engine and reusable bounded-agent runtime.

Its role is to turn repeatable operational work into measurable, human-governed workflows that businesses will pay for now. HausPilot is the first wedge.

The shared runtime should concentrate reusable capabilities:

- capability registry;
- policy/approval gates;
- outcome receipts;
- audit/evals;
- provider adapters.

Vertical packs should supply workflow-specific behavior without forking the authority model.

Commercially, optimise for collected cash **and verified customer value**, not agent count, token volume or maximum automation. A successful bounded workflow can be a paid unit; blocked, failed or shadowed execution is not successful billable work.
