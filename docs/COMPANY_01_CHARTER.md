# Company 01 — Operating Charter

## Purpose

**Make autonomous agents safe enough to do real work for real organisations.**

Company 01 exists to prove that useful autonomous work can scale without giving software unbounded authority.

## Vision

A world where a person, company or public institution can delegate meaningful work to autonomous systems while remaining able to answer, before and after every consequential action:

- Who acted?
- For whom?
- For what purpose?
- Under which delegation?
- What evidence supported the action?
- What could the agent spend or change?
- Did a human need to approve it?
- What actually happened?
- Can the action be traced, contested, revoked or reconciled?

## Company thesis

Models will become abundant. Tools will become abundant. Agents will become abundant.

The scarce capability is **safe delegation of organisational power**.

Company 01 builds the authority layer between autonomous systems and the real world.

## First customer

**Company 01 itself.**

Every internal worker operates through the same Authority Control Plane we intend to sell. Internal work therefore becomes longitudinal product evidence rather than hidden automation.

## First external customer profile

Teams already deploying agents that can read, write, spend, communicate or change real systems and now need stronger operational controls.

Examples:

- AI-native software companies
- operations-heavy SMEs
- regulated teams
- public-sector digital services
- agent-framework and MCP developers

## What we sell first

### 1. Authority Control Plane

A reusable control layer that decides and proves whether an autonomous system may perform a consequential action.

Core capabilities:

- principal + delegation + purpose
- ALLOW / APPROVAL / BLOCK decisions
- spend and action limits
- approved counterparties
- evidence requirements
- exact-bound human approval
- revocation and expiry
- idempotency and replay protection
- failure isolation and reconciliation
- privacy-minimized proof receipts
- progressive autonomy earned from evidence

### 2. Trusted Agent Pilot

A practical first commercial engagement:

1. choose one real agent workflow
2. identify consequential actions and tools
3. define its authority envelope
4. place tools/payment/API calls behind the Authority Gateway
5. run a safety gauntlet
6. run useful real work under bounded authority
7. measure human attention, useful output and invariant violations
8. produce an inspectable proof report

The pilot is successful only if the customer gets useful work **and** the authority invariants remain intact.

### 3. Vertical workers powered by the same control plane

Over time the shared infrastructure powers focused products such as property, legal, healthcare and public-service workflows. The vertical products are evidence that the authority layer generalises; they are not separate trust architectures.

## Company 01 economic engine

Company 01 should create value through a sequence that can begin before the platform is a mature SaaS:

1. **Paid implementation pilots** — help teams safely delegate one valuable workflow.
2. **Hosted Authority Control Plane** — recurring software revenue for policies, receipts, approvals, monitoring and governance.
3. **Usage-based controlled actions** — charge for governed consequential execution at scale where appropriate.
4. **Vertical products** — sell high-value domain workflows on top of the same infrastructure.

Any pricing used before customer discovery is a hypothesis, not evidence.

## What Company 01 does internally

The company uses its own governed digital workforce.

### Human owner

The human retains ultimate organisational authority.

Responsibilities that stay human initially:

- company purpose and strategy
- legal commitments
- material financial commitments
- authority policy changes
- sensitive public statements
- hiring/firing or equivalent people decisions
- approval of autonomy promotions where policy requires it
- emergency revocation / kill switch

### Chief of Staff

Purpose: compress thousands of receipts and events into a small operator queue.

May:

- read authority state
- summarize
- surface exceptions
- recommend priorities
- recommend autonomy promotion or demotion

May not:

- approve consequential actions
- expand authority
- execute consequential actions

### Research Buyer

Purpose: find information and opportunities that improve company decisions.

Work:

- market research
- competitor research
- standards and ecosystem monitoring
- customer and partner research
- grants/tenders/opportunities
- vendor/API/data-source comparison
- paid data acquisition inside a small delegated budget

Tools:

- HTTPS/web research
- search APIs
- MCP tools
- x402/paid-resource APIs
- internal knowledge base

### Engineering workers

Purpose: improve the product and keep the company operational.

Work:

- tests
- bug fixes
- integration work
- documentation
- evaluations
- deployment preparation
- incident investigation

Tools:

- GitHub
- CI
- deployment APIs
- observability
- issue tracker

Merges, production deployment and destructive actions remain separately governed.

### Sales / Customer Ops workers

Purpose: turn product proof into real customers and keep them successful.

Work:

- prospect research
- account enrichment
- prepare outreach
- prepare demos
- summarize calls
- onboarding preparation
- support triage
- renewal signals

External communication begins approval-gated and earns broader authority only through evidence.

### Finance / AP worker

Purpose: keep spending and bills under control.

Work:

- invoice extraction
- invoice matching
- subscription tracking
- expense categorization
- bookkeeping preparation
- bounded routine payment preparation

Bank-detail changes and material payments remain hard approval boundaries.

### Procurement worker

Purpose: obtain approved goods, APIs and services efficiently.

Work:

- collect quotes
- compare vendors
- check approved counterparties
- renew routine services
- perform bounded purchases

### Legal / Governance worker

Purpose: make legal and governance work traceable and evidence-backed.

Work:

- research
- contract comparison
- policy preparation
- provenance checks
- case/workflow preparation

Legally consequential commitments remain approval-gated.

## Initial authority doctrine

1. **Payment is never permission.**
2. **Models may propose authority; models may not grant themselves authority.**
3. **Unknown state fails closed.**
4. **Retries never expand authority.**
5. **A human approval is valid only when bound to the exact action/context.**
6. **A manager agent cannot approve its own recommendations.**
7. **Autonomy is earned from observed evidence and can be removed.**
8. **Consequential execution must be traceable.**
9. **Sensitive payloads do not belong in proof logs.**
10. **Useful work and safety are measured separately; both must improve.**

## North-star question

> Can Company 01 create increasing real economic value while the authority invariants remain true?

## Company scoreboard

Report weekly and do not hide failures.

### Value

- useful work units completed
- customer/revenue opportunities created
- revenue collected
- costs avoided
- operator hours saved
- agent operating cost

### Autonomy

- total proposed actions
- autonomous actions
- human approvals
- blocked actions
- reconciliation cases
- human minutes per 1,000 decisions
- autonomous actions per human intervention
- promotions
- demotions

### Authority invariants

Target:

- unauthorized provider executions = 0
- duplicate consequential executions = 0
- post-revocation executions = 0
- budget invariant violations = 0
- approval bypasses = 0
- missing proof receipts = 0
- sensitive receipt leakage = 0

## First 30-day operating experiment

### Phase 1 — Real Research Buyer

Give the worker real recurring company missions under a tiny budget:

- identify potential design partners
- monitor relevant agent/payments/governance infrastructure
- compare tools and paid data sources
- identify customer pain that can become a Trusted Agent Pilot

No autonomous outbound communication yet.

### Phase 2 — Chief of Staff from real receipts

Generate a daily brief from actual Company 01 activity:

- what useful work completed
- what failed
- what needs human approval
- spend vs budget
- new opportunities
- risk signals
- autonomy promotion/demotion candidates

### Phase 3 — Revenue worker

Add prospecting and pilot-preparation work:

- identify likely customers
- create account briefs
- map their agent/tool consequences
- prepare a tailored Authority Gauntlet
- draft outreach for human approval

Success means at least one real design-partner/pilot conversation generated through the system.

### Phase 4 — Operational worker

Add one worker tied to an actual business burden, chosen from:

- engineering/DevOps
- AP/finance
- procurement
- customer operations

Do not add another worker merely to increase agent count.

## Definition of success

Company 01 is working when all three are true:

1. **Useful:** agents complete work that otherwise consumes human time or produces measurable business value.
2. **Economic:** the company produces real revenue, qualified commercial opportunities or measurable cost/time savings.
3. **Governed:** authority invariants remain intact and autonomy expands only when supported by evidence.

The company itself is the longitudinal proof.
