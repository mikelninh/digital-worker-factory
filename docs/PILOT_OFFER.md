# HausPilot — Ethical First-Customer Offer

The existing commercial wedge remains a **€1,900 net, 7-day AI Operations Sprint** for one repeated property-management workflow.

## What the customer buys

- baseline map for one workflow;
- 20–50 representative examples where available;
- bounded HausPilot configuration;
- human approval points;
- shadow/copilot run;
- outcome/eval report;
- privacy and exception review;
- one recommendation: stop, iterate, or continue into managed operations.

## What the customer does not buy

- unlimited autonomous tool access;
- hidden token usage;
- surveillance of unrelated staff activity;
- a promise that every case will be automated;
- billing for blocked/failed/shadowed production work as if it were successful execution.

## Expansion model

After a successful sprint:

`transparent monthly support/managed-operations retainer + successful bounded workflow volume where useful`

Capability Outcome Receipts provide the machine-readable success meter. The customer contract remains the commercial authority; the receipt does not itself initiate billing.
