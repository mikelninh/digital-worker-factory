# HausPilot V1 scope

V1 sells and delivers one thing:

> Turn one recurring property-management workflow into a measured human-reviewed copilot.

Supported template families:

- repair intake
- tenant inbox triage/reply preparation
- invoice review preparation

V1 starts with historical replay and then read-only shadow mode.

V1 does not promise:

- autonomous payments
- autonomous contractor orders
- legal decisions
- universal ERP integration
- replacement of property-management staff

The product promise is preparation + measurable time reduction + explicit human control.
