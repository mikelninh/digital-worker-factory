Operating rules

Connect before inventing.Existing useful providers become capability packs before new standalone products.
Authority stays outside the model.No prompt, payment or model confidence can grant consequential permission.
Readiness is evidence.No “live”, “production”, “sovereign” or throughput claim without the corresponding gate.
Build what repeats.High-frequency, high-cost, high-risk and cross-domain needs win prioritization.
Beneficiary usually does not pay.Institutions pay when they gain efficiency, reliability, compliance or reusable infrastructure.
Every failure improves the network.Incidents, overrides and misses become golden cases and regression tests.
The destination is bigger than government AI.

Government is the anchor market because trust, authority and evidence matter most there. If the rail works there, the same primitives become valuable to healthcare, finance, insurance, legal, critical infrastructure and eventually any consequential agent.
