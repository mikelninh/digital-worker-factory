The Scorecard result is calculated in the browser. Contact information is not required to receive the result, and the Scorecard is not submitted to Company 01 merely to calculate it.

If you explicitly ask Company 01 to follow up, the service stores your organisation, work email, consent timestamp, sector/agent-stage information, Scorecard answers/results, recommended pilot and funnel status in order to respond to your request and prepare the relevant pilot process.

Abuse prevention

The public lead endpoint processes the caller IP transiently and stores only a SHA-256 hash for rate limiting. Raw IP addresses are not written into the Company 01 growth tables. The rate-limit implementation removes records older than one day during intake.

Legal basis: {{SECURITY_LEGAL_BASIS_AFTER_REVIEW}}

Safe pilot onboarding

Qualified prospects may receive a short-lived onboarding invitation. Company 01 stores only a SHA-256 hash of the invitation token. The token expires after 14 days and can be revoked or replaced.

The onboarding form asks for one workflow, an accountable human owner, system names, desired output and 1–5 synthetic, redacted or otherwise non-sensitive examples. The form explicitly instructs users not to submit production credentials, passwords, access tokens, real client/case records, patient records or other sensitive production data.

Purpose: prepare the requested synthetic/shadow pilot.

Legal basis: {{PILOT_LEGAL_BASIS_AFTER_REVIEW}}
