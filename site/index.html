Why this exists

Agents have tools. Organisations still need authority.

Identity tells you who the actor is. MCP and APIs let it call tools. Payment rails let it move value. None of those answer whether this exact action is institutionally authorised right now.

FAQ

Simple questions first.

Is this another agent framework?

No. Agent frameworks decide how work is reasoned about and orchestrated. Company 01 focuses on whether the exact consequence is authorised before it reaches the tool or system.

Does every action need human approval?

No. That would defeat the point. Routine work should be autonomous inside explicit limits. Human attention is reserved for exceptions, sensitive access and material consequences.

Do we need production data to test it?

No. The first pilot is designed to work with synthetic, redacted or non-sensitive examples, then move to shadow and production only when the evidence supports it.

Is this a legal or regulatory certification?

No. The current product is authority infrastructure and a reference proof system. Sector-specific legal and compliance obligations remain the organisation's responsibility.

AI can act. Authority must remain outside the model.
