What is truly happening?

The AI is doing a real piece of office work: data → decision-ready summary.

The point is not the animated steps. The point is that a person would otherwise open four sources, copy numbers, notice patterns and write the same weekly update by hand.

Before

A person assembles the report manually.

They open separate tools, copy facts into a document, compare them and decide what is important enough to mention.

Orderssales + delayed shipments
Supportrepeated customer complaints
Inventorystock vs restock threshold
Returnsrepeated return reasons
After

The team receives one useful brief.

The worker collects the permitted facts, reconciles overlapping signals and produces a short report with concrete priorities. A human can inspect the inputs and decide what to do.

Where human control matters

Preparing is automatic. Acting outside the team can wait for approval.

The same worker pattern can prepare an external action without silently executing it. That is the second idea Digital Worker Factory demonstrates.

AI can prepare“17 customers are affected by the delayed batch. Here is the drafted update.”

Context gathering and drafting are repetitive work.

For the technical reviewer

The simple business example sits on stricter controls underneath.
