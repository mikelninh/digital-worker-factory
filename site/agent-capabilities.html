What it does

Three things. That’s the whole idea.

Digital Worker Factory is for repetitive work where someone currently has to collect information, turn it into something useful, and then decide what happens next.

1. Gather

It reads the information it is allowed to use and brings the relevant pieces together.

2. Prepare

It does the repetitive part: reconcile, summarize, draft or structure the next step.

3. Ask

If the next action matters — sending, changing or publishing something — it waits for a person.

Why teams care

Less copy-paste. Fewer handoffs. Clearer control.

The goal is not “autonomous AI”. It is to remove the boring coordination work while keeping important decisions understandable and human-controlled.
