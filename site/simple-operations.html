A case comes in. The system uses only the tools it is allowed to use, keeps the evidence attached, prepares the work — and leaves the consequential decision with the qualified human.

The same five steps every time
1. Case comes inDocuments, messages or structured data.
2. Use allowed toolsOnly capabilities enabled for this role.
3. Check evidenceClaims stay tied to their source.
4. Prepare the workA reviewable answer, draft or next action.
5. Human decidesImportant actions do not bypass approval.
