Pre-public validation · evidence first

Trust should be a claim you can inspect.

OCN provides verifiable assurance for specific claims in a specific context at a specific time. It does not issue a magical global trust score. Every proof card says what is verified, how strong the evidence is, what is still unknown, and what must happen before the claim is promoted.

FORMAL · BOUNDEDPRODUCTION KERNEL

Authority & preflight state-space check

Exhaustively checks the finite model of the production deterministic authority/preflight functions.

  • Explicit matching grants are the only authority source.
  • Payment never grants authority.
  • Stale or insufficient evidence cannot be allowed.
  • Write/consequential work cannot be allowed without human approval in v1.
  • review/block do not execute in the mediator model.

Not proven: external-world truth, arbitrary LLM correctness, every downstream adapter’s equivalence to the abstract mediator.

  • Settlement succeeded.
  • Bounded service executed.
  • Payment and authority remained separate.
  • Request/output receipt hashes were produced.

Not proven: mainnet revenue, external repeat demand, production throughput.

What we refuse to “prove”

No universal trust score

Context changes. Evidence expires. A system can be strong on authority and weak on substantive truth. OCN preserves those dimensions instead of collapsing them into 93/100.

Unknown stays unknown

If identity, evidence, source truth, policy applicability or outcome has not been verified, the envelope says unknown, insufficient_evidence or review.

Promotion gates

Assurance is earned with evidence.

M2 / M3 · PublicStable HTTPS + live proof cards + external settlements.
A4 · OperationalReal outcomes, override/error rates, uptime and latency.
A5 · IndependentRelevant external security/compliance/formal attestation. Never self-awarded.
The product promise: Before your agent acts, OCN checks the evidence, authority, freshness and policy. After it acts, OCN leaves proof of what happened.
